# E C Dunn Ltd — Capability Statement

*Specialist ceilings and partitions contractor. 25+ years trading. London, Essex, and the wider South East of England.*

---

## Who we are

E C Dunn Ltd has been fitting suspended ceilings, partitions, and drylining across London and the South East since 2001. Over 25 years and 500+ completed projects, our work has become trusted in sectors where **compliance, acoustics, and finish matter more than speed** — schools, hospitals, heritage buildings, and upscale commercial fit-outs.

We are a **trade-delivery contractor**, not a main contractor. We partner with principal contractors, architects, M&E firms, and facilities teams who need a reliable specialist for the ceilings-and-partitions scope within a wider fit-out.

## Core specialisms

### Schools & Education (40+ delivered)
Acoustic ceiling systems, corridor partitions, hall renovations. Compliance-critical work built to last across London and Essex schools.

- **Acoustic compliance** — BB93 sound-insulation standards
- **BBA certified** systems
- **DfE approved** for school estates work

### Hospitals & Healthcare (NHS)
Fire-rated, hygienic ceiling systems for NHS trusts, clinics, and healthcare facilities. Precision-grade work meeting HTM compliance.

- **HTM-compliant** installation (HTM 60, HTM 63 etc.)
- **Fire-rated Cat A** systems
- **Antibacterial** finish ratings where specified

### Partitions & Drylining (core service)
Metal stud partitions, drylining, and acoustic separations for offices, schools, hospitals, and commercial refurbishments. The most in-demand trade in the business.

- **Metal stud & MF** systems
- **Acoustic-rated** to project spec
- **Fire compartmentation** to Building Regulations

---

## Full services list

### Suspended Ceilings
Full installation of metal grid and mineral fibre tile systems. Work with all major manufacturers including **Armstrong, Rockfon, and Knauf**. Clean, professional ceiling solutions for schools, offices, healthcare, and commercial premises.

### MF Ceilings
Metal frame (MF) ceiling systems for seamless, flush-finished ceilings. Ideal for high-end commercial spaces, restaurants, and heritage restoration projects where tile-grid ceilings would be visually inappropriate.

### Partitions & Drylining
Stud wall partitions, plasterboard drylining, acoustic separation for offices, schools, and commercial buildings. Built to spec, fire-rated options available.

### Fire-Rated Systems
Specialist fire-rated ceilings and partition installations meeting current Building Regulations. Essential for schools, hospitals, and public buildings. Compartmentation work and passive fire protection.

### Heritage & Restoration
Sensitive ceiling and partition work in listed buildings, heritage sites, and period properties. Reversible installation methods where required, matching to period finishes, coordination with conservation officers.
