## Priority 2: Tender Platforms (Government & Public Sector Work)

Your dad has done schools and hospitals — this is where the BIG contracts live.

### Contracts Finder (GOV.UK)
- URL: https://www.contractsfinder.service.gov.uk/
- WHY: All UK public sector contracts over £12,000 must be advertised here
- Search terms: "suspended ceilings", "partitions", "drylining", "ceiling works"
- Filter by: South East England, London
- Set up email alerts for new tenders matching his keywords

### CompeteFor
- URL: https://www.competefor.com/
- WHY: Supply chain opportunities for major projects (Olympics legacy, Crossrail successor projects, HS2 subcontracts)
- Register with trades: Ceilings, Partitions, Drylining

### ProContract / In-Tend
- URL: varies by council
- WHY: Many local councils use these for smaller works
- Register with: Essex County Council, London Borough of Havering, and other local authorities
- Search: "ceiling", "partition", "internal fit-out"

### Supply2Gov
- URL: https://www.supply2gov.uk/
- WHY: Aggregates public sector tenders, sends email alerts
- Free basic account

### Construction Line
- URL: https://www.constructionline.co.uk/
- WHY: Pre-qualification system — being registered here means you can bid on more tenders
- Cost: ~£300-500/year but WORTH IT for public sector work
- Your dad's 25-year track record makes this an easy qualification

## Ongoing Lead Gen Strategy

### Supply Chain Referral System
Your dad's biggest asset is that his supply chain remembers him. Formalise this:
- Call his top 5 suppliers and tell them he's actively taking on work
- Ask if they can refer him when other contractors ask for ceiling/partition recommendations
- Offer to be their "go-to" subcontractor referral
- Consider a simple referral fee (£50-100 per won job) for anyone who sends work his way

### Direct Outreach to Main Contractors
- Identify main contractors doing fit-outs in London/Essex
- Send a simple capabilities email with:
  - Company name & registration
  - Years in business
  - Types of work
  - Areas covered
  - Contact details
- Target: Fit-out contractors, office refurbishment companies, school renovation specialists
