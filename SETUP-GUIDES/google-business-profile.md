# Google Business Profile Setup — E C Dunn Ltd

## Why This Matters
Google Business Profile (GBP) is the single highest-impact free marketing tool for a local trades business. When someone searches "ceilings contractor near me" or "suspended ceilings Hornchurch", your GBP listing appears in the map pack — the top 3 results with a map that get ~44% of all clicks.

Your dad has ZERO online presence right now. Setting this up puts him on the map — literally.

---

## Step-by-Step Setup

### 1. Go to Google Business Profile
- Visit: https://business.google.com
- Sign in with a Google account (create one for the business if needed: ecdunnltd@gmail.com)

### 2. Add Your Business
- Click "Add your business to Google"
- Business name: **E C Dunn Ltd**
- Business category: **Suspended ceiling contractor** (primary)
  - Additional categories to add:
    - Dry wall contractor
    - Partition wall contractor
    - Ceiling contractor
    - Interior construction contractor
- Business type: **Service area business** (he goes to clients, not vice versa)

### 3. Set Service Areas
Add these areas:
- London (all boroughs)
- Hornchurch
- Romford
- Essex
- Kent
- South East England
- Dartford
- Chelmsford
- Basildon
- Brentwood

### 4. Contact Details
- Phone: [Dad's actual phone number]
- Website: https://ecdunnltd.co.uk (once deployed)
- Business address: Spectrum House, 2b Suttons Lane, Hornchurch, Essex, RM12 6RJ
  - Tick "I deliver goods and services to my customers"
  - Choose to HIDE the address if he doesn't want people visiting the office

### 5. Verification
Google will verify via:
- **Postcard** (takes 5-14 days) — most common for new businesses
- **Phone call** (sometimes offered)
- **Video verification** (may be offered — walk around the office/van with branding)

### 6. Optimise the Profile (DO THIS — most people skip it)

#### Business Description (750 chars max):
```
E C Dunn Ltd is a specialist ceilings and partitions contractor with over 25 years of experience across London, Essex, and South East England. We provide expert installation of suspended ceilings, partitions, drylining, MF ceilings, fire-rated systems, and heritage ceiling restoration. Our skilled teams have completed hundreds of projects in schools, hospitals, restaurants, heritage buildings, and commercial properties. Family-run and trusted by our supply chain partners for over two decades. Contact us for a free, no-obligation quote.
```

#### Services to Add:
- Suspended ceiling installation
- Partition wall installation
- Drylining
- MF ceiling installation
- Fire-rated ceiling systems
- Fire-rated partition systems
- Heritage ceiling restoration
- Bulkhead installation
- Ceiling raft installation
- Commercial ceiling fit-out
- Office partition installation
- School ceiling renovation
- Hospital ceiling installation

#### Attributes:
- Identifies as family-owned: Yes
- Established year: 2001

#### Photos to Upload (when available):
- Logo
- Cover photo (best completed project)
- 5-10 project photos across different sectors
- Team at work photos
- Before/after shots

### 7. Get First Reviews
This is CRITICAL. Ask these people for a Google review:
- Long-standing supply chain contacts who know his work
- Recent satisfied clients
- His brother (if appropriate)
- Any repeat clients

Send them this link (after profile is live):
```
https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=[PLACE_ID]
```
(You'll get the Place ID from the GBP dashboard)

Template message to send:
```
Hi [Name], Edward here from E C Dunn Ltd. We've just set up our Google page and would really appreciate a quick review if you've been happy with our work. It would mean a lot — here's the link: [LINK]. Thanks!
```

### 8. Post Regularly
Google rewards active profiles. Post:
- Project completion photos (weekly if possible)
- "Now taking bookings for [month]"
- Seasonal messages
- Any offers or availability updates
