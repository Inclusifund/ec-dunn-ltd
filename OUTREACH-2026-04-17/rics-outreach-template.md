# RICS Building Surveyor Outreach

> **Target:** RICS-registered Chartered Building Surveyors in Essex, London and South East. They write schedules of dilapidations and recommend 3 contractors per job to the client. Being on their recommendation list = automatic invites with zero competition bid cost.
>
> **Why this works for Edward:** 25+ years of clean handovers + Essex local + HTM/BBA credentials = exactly what a surveyor wants to recommend without risking their reputation. Surveyors pick people who won't embarrass them.
>
> **Cadence:** Email 1 → Coffee ask · Coffee → Leave capability PDF + rate card · 90-day follow-up check-in.
>
> **Volume:** 50 surveyors over 6 weeks. One coffee per working day. Edward drives, Reece books.

---

## How to build the target list (one-off, 2 hours)

1. **RICS Find a Surveyor directory** — https://www.ricsfirms.com/
   - Service: *Building surveys and dilapidations*
   - Locations: Chelmsford, Romford, Ilford, Brentwood, Basildon, Southend, Dartford, Bromley, Canary Wharf, City of London, West End, Croydon, Sutton, Kingston, Twickenham, Guildford, Reigate, Crawley, Brighton, Maidstone, Sevenoaks, Tunbridge Wells
   - Filter: chartered firms (RICS-regulated)
2. **LinkedIn cross-reference** — search `Chartered Building Surveyor` + location. Pull 50 with active posting history (means they reply to emails).
3. **QS firms worth adding** — Gleeds, Arcadis, Faithful+Gould, Gardiner & Theobald regional offices. They often control sub lists on larger jobs.

Target count: 50 surveyors. Spread across 25 firms (often 2 surveyors per firm is fine — coffee one, email the other as "your colleague mentioned").

## Coffee meeting prep — what Edward should bring

1. **Printed capability statement** (the PDF, 1 page)
2. **Printed rate card** — day rates for fixers + sq-m rates for MF ceilings, suspended ceilings, partitions, drylining (to create separately)
3. **Photo portfolio on phone** — 8–10 hero shots from completed jobs (Edward has these on his phone, just curate)
4. **One anecdote ready** — the "we got called back to fix another firm's failed job" or "we restored a 1920s plaster ceiling in the City" story. Every surveyor remembers the person, not the price.
5. **His business card** — needs updating if not already in EC Dunn branding

## Coffee meeting — what to ask (15 minutes)

- "What kind of dilapidations jobs do you see most often in [their region]?"
- "When a client asks you for 3 contractors on a ceiling/partition scope, what makes you pick one over another — beyond price?"
- "Any pet peeves from subs you've recommended in the past?"
- "If I send you a schedule every quarter with completed project photos, would that be useful or noise?"

Edward listens, doesn't pitch. The ask is the recommendation list, not the sale.
